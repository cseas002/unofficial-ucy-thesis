\chapter {Research Methodology}
\label{label6}

This section describes the materials and methods used for the research. It is essential to describe the steps clearly and accurately to enable readers to determine how much your methodology may have affected your results and to replicate your study if necessary. This allows them to evaluate your research's reliability and validity and dissertation topic.

A methods section may include an overview of the research design, details of the sample and the sampling technique used, and a description of procedures, materials, and statistical tools.
